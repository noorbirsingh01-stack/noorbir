Privacy Policy & Data Ethics

LAST REVISED: OCTOBER 2026 // EFFECTIVE DATE: IMMEDIATE

1. Zero Invasive Tracking Declaration

At noorbir.co, we maintain a strict engineering stance against invasive behavioral surveillance. We do not employ third-party session recorders (e.g. Hotjar, FullStory), keystroke trackers, or cross-site advertising cookies. Your interactions on this site are private, sovereign, and clean.

2. Self-Hosted Privacy Infrastructure

All fonts, stylesheets, and scripts are bundled locally or through privacy-respecting content networks. Zero IP addresses or client telemetry are transmitted to third-party ad networks.

3. Data Collection & Business Inquiries

We collect personal identification data (Name, Business Email, Corporate Domain) strictly when you voluntarily submit an inquiry for a technical system audit. This data is utilized solely for:

  • Performing requested response-time and infrastructure evaluations.
  • Delivering confidential technical reports and proposals.
  • Executing contractual engineering deliverables.

4. Bot Mitigation & Spam Defense

Inquiry endpoints are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, an enterprise-grade cryptographic challenge designed to verify human visitors without harvesting personal telemetry or reading third-party cookies.

5. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, audit deletion requests, or technical data clarifications, reach our engineering desk directly at founder@noorbir.co.
